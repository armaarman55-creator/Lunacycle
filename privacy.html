Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Who we are

BloomCycle is a period and cycle tracking app provided by Diswayne Tech Systems ("we", "us").

If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at support@getdiswayne.com.

What we collect

Account details: your name and email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.

Health information you choose to enter: period start and end dates, flow, moods, symptoms, notes, intimacy logs (including whether protection was used), your life stage, pregnancy or birth dates, and your cycle settings.

We only collect what you type into the app. Everything in the health category is optional.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or browsing activity. What the ad service collects in the free version is described under Advertising.

How we use your data

We use your data only to run the app for you: to show your history, calculate predictions and insights, schedule reminders on your device, and restore your data when you sign in on another device.

We do not sell your data. The health information you enter is never shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Where your data is stored

On your device, in the app's private storage.

In Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which we use for sign-in and cloud backup. Google stores this data on our behalf and encrypts it in transit and at rest. Google's servers may be located outside your country.

Access rules limit each account to its own data.

Advertising

The free version of BloomCycle shows ads from Google AdMob. Subscribers see no ads, and the app does not load the ad service for them.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, general device details and how you interact with ads. Google's privacy policy explains how it uses this information.

We ask Google for ads that are not personalised from your activity, and where the law requires it we ask for your consent before any ad is shown.

We never send the health information you enter (periods, moods, symptoms, notes, intimacy logs or pregnancy details) to the ad service.

Subscription and payments

The optional subscription that removes ads is sold and billed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether the subscription is active.

Other services the app contacts

The app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your device's IP address, as with any internet request. No health or account data is sent.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Their content is not sent to us. Notification text can be visible on your lock screen; you can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it.

You can edit or delete individual entries in the app at any time.

You can delete your account in the app: open Profile, then choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your cloud data and the data stored by the app on that device.

If you cannot use the app, email support@getdiswayne.com from your account's email address and we will delete your account and data.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law. Contact support@getdiswayne.com and we will respond.

Children

BloomCycle is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We limit access to your data and rely on Google Firebase's security for cloud storage. No system is perfectly secure. Protect your account with a strong password and your phone with a screen lock, because anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app.

Not medical advice

BloomCycle is not a medical device. Predictions are estimates based on the dates you log and can be wrong. Do not use the app as contraception or to diagnose or treat any condition. Speak to a healthcare professional about any health concern.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

How to delete your account

In the app, open Profile, choose Delete account, and confirm with your password. Your account and data are removed straight away.

If you no longer have the app, email support@getdiswayne.com from your account's email address and we will delete your account and all its data.

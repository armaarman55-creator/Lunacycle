How to install

Takes less than a minute

1

Download the APK

Tap the download button above to save the APK file to your phone.

2

Open the file

Tap the download notification, or find app-release.apk in your Files app under Downloads.

3

Allow & install

If Android asks, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back and tap Install.

Android may show a Play Protect notice because Lunacycle is installed outside the Play Store. On Android 7 and older, the setting is under Settings → Security → Unknown sources.
